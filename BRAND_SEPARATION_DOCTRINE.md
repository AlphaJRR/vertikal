# BRAND SEPARATION DOCTRINE
**Status:** LOCKED | **Effective Date:** January 2026  
**Authority:** Strategic Review | **Review Cycle:** Quarterly

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

**Vertikal is the future. AVA is credibility ballast.**

This is not a sunset. This is not a merge. This is **strategic separation with preserved equity**.

---

## DOCTRINE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### ✅ DO
- **Vertikal:** Market, build, scale, invest
- **AVA:** Maintain as archive, reference for track record, optional licensing IP
- **Separation:** Keep clean, documented, reversible
- **Audit Trail:** Every change committed, every decision documented

### ❌ DON'T
- **Do not market AVA** (it's not the growth brand)
- **Do not sunset AVA** (it's valuable equity)
- **Do not merge AVA back** (separation is intentional)
- **Do not create brand ambiguity** (one parent platform: Vertikal)

---

## STRATEGIC POSITIONING

### Vertikal Media Company LLC
**Role:** Primary platform brand  
**Purpose:** Growth, investment, market presence  
**Status:** Active development, marketing, scaling

### Alpha Visual Artists (AVA)
**Role:** Legacy / archive / credibility layer  
**Purpose:** Track record proof, optional licensing, historical IP  
**Status:** Maintained, not marketed, preserved

---

## IMPLEMENTATION STANDARDS

### Code Standards
- ✅ Zero AVA references in active code
- ✅ All branding: Vertikal Media Company LLC
- ✅ Footer: Official LLC footer on all pages
- ✅ Legal: Terms/Privacy pages live

### Business Standards
- ✅ Marketing: 100% Vertikal branding
- ✅ Investor materials: Vertikal as primary
- ✅ Legal documents: Vertikal Media Company LLC
- ✅ AVA: Referenced only as historical context

### Documentation Standards
- ✅ All changes: Git committed with clear messages
- ✅ All decisions: Documented in strategic recaps
- ✅ All separations: Reversible (no destructive changes)
- ✅ All audits: Grep-verifiable

---

## REVERSIBILITY PRINCIPLE

**Every separation must be reversible.**

This means:
- No destructive deletions
- Clear separation points
- Clear rollback procedures
- Documented decision rationale

**Why:** Strategic flexibility. If market conditions change, we can pivot without technical debt.

---

## AUDIT REQUIREMENTS

### Quarterly Review
1. Verify zero AVA references in active code
2. Confirm Vertikal branding consistency
3. Review legal compliance (footer, Terms, Privacy)
4. Assess separation effectiveness

### Before Major Releases
1. Brand consistency check
2. Legal compliance verification
3. Separation integrity audit
4. Documentation update

---

## EXCEPTIONS & EDGE CASES

### When AVA Can Be Referenced
- Historical context in investor materials
- Track record documentation
- Licensing discussions (if applicable)
- Archive access (if implemented)

### When AVA Cannot Be Referenced
- Active marketing materials
- Primary brand identity
- User-facing copy
- Investor pitch (except historical context)
