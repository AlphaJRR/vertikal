Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a session?

Navigate to the "Book" tab, select a package, choose your date and time, and complete payment for the deposit.

When will I receive my deliverables?

Delivery timelines vary by package. You'll receive updates in your client portal as your project progresses.

Can I request revisions?

Yes! Revision policies are included in each package. Additional revisions may be available for an additional fee.

How do I review my video?

When a deliverable is ready for review, you'll see it in your client portal. Click "Review" to add timestamped comments.
