RULE OF THIRDS

Place your subject on a third, not the center, unless you have a reason.

WHY IT MATTERS

  • The rule of thirds divides your frame into a 3×3 grid for natural balance.
  • Placing eyes, horizons, or key objects on the intersections creates tension and interest.
  • Use the opposite third for negative space to guide the viewer's eye.
