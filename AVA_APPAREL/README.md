## 📋 USAGE GUIDELINES

### **PHOTO_SELECTS/**
- Raw selected photos organized by line
- Use selection rules from master spreadsheet
- One folder per line (VISIONARY, CREATOR, ALPHA_KREW, STORYTELLER)

### **MOCKUPS/**
- Photoshop/ChatGPT generated mockups
- Organized by line
- Final mockups ready for product pages

### **PRODUCT_IMAGES/**
- Final product photography
- Ready for e-commerce upload
- Multiple angles per SKU

### **LOOKBOOK/**
- Complete lookbook layouts
- Page-by-page designs
- Print-ready files

### **DROP_001_FINAL/**
- Locked final assets
- Approved images only
- Ready for production/launch

## 🎯 SELECTION RULES (QUICK REFERENCE)

### **VISIONARY**
- Clean portraits
- Neutral walls
- Calm body language
- Daylight / soft key
- Minimal color
- *Rule: If it feels loud, it's wrong.*

### **CREATOR**
- Cars (rolling, headlights, interiors)
- City at night
- Flash photography
- Motion blur
- Walking, turning, movement
- Bright highlights on black
- *Rule: If it doesn't feel fast, cut it.*

### **ALPHA KREW**
- Group shots
- On-set wides
- Crew working
- Leadership posture
- Uniform clothing
- *Rule: Authority over personality.*

### **STORYTELLER**
- Interviews
- Off-frame eye lines
- Quiet moments
- Emotion > aesthetics
- *Rule: Human first, brand second.*

## 🎨 PHOTOSHOP PROMPTS

### **VISIONARY**
> Edit this image for the AVA Visionary apparel line. Maintain a minimal, premium, future-forward look. Neutralize colors, reduce visual noise, clean blacks, soft highlights, subtle contrast increase. Preserve natural skin tones. High-end luxury brand aesthetic. No saturation boost.

### **CREATOR**
> Edit this image for the AVA Creator apparel line. Emphasize bold color contrast, dynamic energy, and speed. Increase saturation selectively on highlights, deepen blacks, enhance motion blur or light streaks if present, sharpen subject slightly. Flash photography feel encouraged. Image should feel fast, expressive, and powerful while remaining premium.

### **ALPHA KREW**
> Edit this image for the AVA Alpha Krew apparel line. Focus on authority and unity. Desaturate colors slightly, increase contrast, emphasize structure and depth. Cinematic workwear aesthetic. Strong shadows, controlled highlights. Professional and dominant tone.

### **STORYTELLER**
> Edit this image for the AVA Storyteller apparel line. Prioritize emotion and narrative. Soft contrast, gentle highlights, natural tones. Optional subtle grain. Preserve authenticity and human connection. Cinematic documentary feel. Avoid heavy stylization.
